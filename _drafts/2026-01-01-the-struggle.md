2025 was not a good year for my business. It  

After 4 years of steady growth, I made an error that took me over 5 months to fix. During these months, my revenue flat-lined and then declined. It was extremely unpleasant for me personally, but it did force me to confront (and hoepfully fix) some unpleasant truths. 

But let's begin at the beginning.

## Before 2025: Wow, this is easy!

I began working on my product full-time in March 2021. You can read all about how and why I got started [here](/blog/becoming-an-indie-hacker/). Revenue was unspectacular at first (around 450 USD per month) but it grew, month over month. Revenue continued to grow at a healthy rate of 300% year-on-year. It even looked like growth was accelerating.

I was faintly worried that it just seemed too easy. There were so many stories of founders facing "The Struggle" or their "crucible" moment -- where was mine? The worst that I had experienced was going offline for a few hours once. Another time I messed up a live sports competition. Unpleasant, sure, but hardly seared into my memory forever.

Did this mean it was still ahead of me, I wondered. Yes. Yes it did.
