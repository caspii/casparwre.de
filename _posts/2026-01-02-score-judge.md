## 🚀 Yet another product!

At the end of 2025 I split my product into two: Keepthescore.com got a sister site called [leaderboarded.com](https://leaderboarded.com). You can read more about the reasons behind it [here](/blog/splitting-keepthescore/).

Now I have created a third product: [Scorejudge.com](https://Scorejudge.com). This new product is an experiment (like everything else).

## What can Scorejudge do?

 Scorejudge allows you to create a competition with an attached leaderboard. The competition could be a hackathon,  a dance contest, or a painting contest. The central idea is that the competition has a number of entries (e.g. teams or paintings) and a number of people judging the entries. Scorejudge makes this process seamless by allowing you to:

1. Provide each judge with their own individual link. Judges do **not** require a login to take part. The unique link suffices.
2. Each judge can then give points according to a number of predefined criteria.
3. The points (or scores) are automatically tallied on a leaderboard which can be shown publicly or embedded on a website.

## Why did I make this?

I launched it for 2 reasons:

1. The rise of AI has made it ridiculously easy to ~~launch a product~~ code a product.
2. Users of leaderboarded.com have asked for this use-case quite frequently.

Since I was getting a little burnt out with running my existing products (now both over 4 years old), I wanted to try something new. And instead of building in a niche that was wildy different to my existing products, it made sense to try this in an adjacent field.
