Why are lacrosse scoreboards so hard to find? Physical scoreboards cost thousands of dollars, and most youth leagues and club teams make do with a whiteboard or a phone timer. Of course you can download dedicated scorekeeping software — but those solutions come with their own headaches:

*   They only run on Windows
*   They have terrible usability
*   They're overkill for a single game on a Tuesday afternoon
*   They look awful

So here's a better option: _a lacrosse scoreboard that runs in your browser_. It looks like this:

!['lacrosse scoreboard'](/images/lacrosse-scoreboard.png){:class="img-responsive"}

No download required. It works on any device with a browser — laptop, tablet, or phone. You can control the score from the sideline on your phone while it displays on a laptop connected to a TV or projector.

It supports everything you need for a lacrosse game: goals, quarters, a shot clock, possession indicator, and timeout tracking. It works for field lacrosse, box lacrosse, and women's lacrosse.

And if you're streaming the game with OBS or Streamlabs, you can use it as a live scoreboard overlay — just copy the browser source URL.
